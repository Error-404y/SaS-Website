Troop Training

1 Choose troops2 Enter point target3 See required troops, costs & time

Troops needed for your point target

Origin Time comes from the troop screenshots. Actual Time uses the training time you see in-game, which may be shorter. Phase 3 scoring: Level IX = 1,485 points and Level X = 1,960 points per troop.

How is training time calculated?

The screenshot Origin Time is for 722 troops. Actual Time is calculated from the actual 722-troop duration you enter for the selected troop type. Both times scale with the number of troops. Because the available screenshots do not establish every actual time, no reduction is assumed. With Training Capacity UP enabled, the number of troops may be rounded up to a complete threefold batch.
