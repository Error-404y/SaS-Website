How are these numbers calculated?

The screenshots show rounded costs and training time for 722 troops. Other quantities are proportional estimates, not guaranteed in-game totals. With the optional boost ON, RSS and time are doubled (2×) as requested. The pictured boost costs 3K Diamonds.
